# REWIND RAMPAGE

> **The past was never erased.**

**REWIND RAMPAGE** is a fast-paced, neon-styled 2D action game created for **Ludum Dare 60**, inspired by the theme **Old Tech**.

Move, leave a trail, and rewind through your own movements to fight back against relentless pursuers. But the machine is recording more than your survival: every second you leave behind may become something you have to face.

## The Game

Inside a malfunctioning, tape-based temporal system, time behaves like an old recording. It can be recorded, rewound, and played back—but it cannot always be erased.

The game unfolds in three acts:

### Act I — RECORD

**Survive for 90 seconds.**

Enemy pursuers continue to spawn as the pressure rises. Move across the arena, create your own path, and use rewind to turn your movements into attacks. During the final 30 seconds, the system records your actions for what comes next.

*Timer: `00:00 → 01:30` (counting up).*

### Act II — REWIND

**Hunt your past self in 30 seconds.**

A phantom replays the final 30 seconds of your Act I run. While you try to intercept it, a barrage of projectiles is fired toward its position, forcing you to fight inside the chaos of your own recorded survival.

*Timer: `00:00 → 00:30` (counting up).*

### Act III — ERASE

**Face the copies that were never deleted.**

The archive releases independent echoes of you. Unlike the recorded phantom, these copies move freely and launch their own rewind attacks. Destroy the time core and stay alive until the purge countdown reaches zero.

*Timer: `01:30 → 00:00` (counting down).*

## Core Mechanics

- **Move and trace:** Your movement leaves a path through the arena.
- **Rewind to attack:** Retrace your route at high speed and use your recorded movement offensively.
- **Exploit intersections:** Use intersecting trails to trigger powerful attacks.
- **Fight your own history:** Your recorded actions become the opponent in Act II.
- **Survive the archive:** In Act III, copies of you can rewind and attack independently.

## Visual Style & Theme

The game combines a dark 2D grid arena, cyan movement trails, red pursuers, and glowing geometric effects with the look of vintage electronic displays.

The **Old Tech** theme is built into the world: the time system operates like an aging magnetic-tape recorder. Its failed erase mechanism leaves behind copies that should no longer exist.
