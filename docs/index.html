STORY MODE

ACT I — RECORD

Survive. Every movement matters.

Survive for 90 seconds against relentless pursuers. Your movements are recorded, and the final 30 seconds will become your next challenge.

After 01:00, the arena clock turns red and displays milliseconds as the recording enters its final moments.

ACT II — REWIND

Face the person you were 30 seconds ago.

Your recorded self replays the final 30 seconds of Act I at its original speed.

Follow its purple one-second path preview, dodge incoming fire, and intercept your past self with a single rewind attack.

The phantom's own recorded attacks cannot hurt you. But the system's projectiles certainly can.

ACT III — ERASE

Some recordings refuse to disappear.

The archive releases the copies that should have been erased.

With only 1 HP remaining, survive their attacks. When the time core appears with 30 seconds left, destroy it with three successful hits.

Breaking the core isn't enough.

You must survive until 00:00.

ENDLESS MODE — THE TAPE NEVER STOPS

How long can you survive when even the battlefield can rewind?

Begin with three challenges:

  • BULLET STORM — Survive the pursuing swarm.
  • DETONATION — Rewind and replay a chain of explosions.
  • MECHANIC RUSH — Learn the rules of the battlefield.

Then face MASTER TAPE, an enormous, indestructible cassette that turns the entire arena against you.

Track locks, sweeping beams, exploding sectors, and advancing danger zones combine into increasingly demanding attacks.

Rewinding also reverses the arena's recorded history. Every mechanism can play again, and its attacks can destroy your pursuers.

But the machine has learned to rewind, too.

MEMORY LOCK forces you backward through your own movements, then makes you face the same attack without advance warnings.

After the first cycle, every boss phase combines multiple mechanics and ends with another memory challenge. Each spawn releases more pursuers, eventually reaching 81 at once.

The boss cannot be destroyed. The tape keeps running.

Your only goal is to last longer.

CHEAT KEYS & CHALLENGE SHORTCUTS

These optional shortcuts let you explore the game, experiment with its mechanics, or jump straight into its toughest challenges.

F2 — GOD MODE

Toggle invincibility ON / OFF. Disabled by default.

F4 — ENDLESS MODE SHORTCUT

Enter Endless Mode without completing the story first.

F8 — MASTER TAPE CHALLENGE

Think you're ready for the hardest part?

Jump directly into MASTER TAPE's first-loop final trial (P7), where advanced boss mechanics put your movement, timing, and memory to the test.

God Mode stays OFF. No protection. Just you and the tape.
